# Sample photograph

`photobooth-sample.jpg` is an original AI-generated photograph of fictional adult friends, created with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-17. It is a demonstration asset, not a customer photograph or testimonial. The JPEG is a format conversion of the generated PNG. No third-party image service is contacted at runtime.

The landing page applies Photoshoot's actual GLSL shaders to this source image. Effects are not baked into the photograph. The source is copied to a content-hashed filename by `web/build-web.mjs`; the page preloads it and falls back to a procedural sample if loading fails.

The user requested a quieter, more cohesive photographic direction after finding the abstract backdrop artwork out of place. The resulting direction uses candid photobooth portraits, neutral clothing and a gray curtain. The app's procedural backdrops remain in a smaller, separate preview row.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original sample photograph used as the source texture for real GLSL photobooth effects on a website. Create ONE landscape 3:2 photograph, full bleed, absolutely no collage, no panels, no borders, no lettering, no UI, no special effects, no watermark. Subject: two fictional adult friends around age 25 sitting closely side by side in a small photo booth, shoulders and heads visible, looking directly at the lens. Left: a woman with naturally wavy dark brown shoulder-length hair, simple pale gray cotton t-shirt, genuine amused open smile. Right: a man with short slightly curly dark hair and a plain dark charcoal crewneck shirt, a playful scrunched-nose smile. Natural everyday people, not polished fashion models. Heads both entirely inside frame with generous space above and on either side; faces located around one-third and two-thirds across. Background: a plain neutral medium-gray fabric photo-booth curtain with subtle folds, low contrast, no furnishings or props. Lighting: soft direct booth flash, natural skin texture, candid warmth, subtle photographic grain, realistic color, gentle soft shadows. Mood: spontaneous shared laugh from an actual webcam photobooth session. Restrained clothing and background so expressions are the focus; no bright colors. Sharp, natural, believable photography. This will be cropped to an 8:5 frame, so keep both faces within the center 80 percent of the image.
